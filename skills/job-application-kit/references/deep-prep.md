# Win Pack: deep preparation for a job the user really wants

The goal is for the user to know the job, the company and the interview better than most candidates. Run it when the user says "prep me", "win pack", or asks for company or interview research. It is on request so the default flow stays fast. Offer it after every package for the jobs that matter.

## Contents (each claim sourced and dated; unknown stays "unknown")
1. **Company dossier:** what they do and sell, customers, size and stage, funding or financial health where public, products, technology they use (from their own pages, engineering blogs, job posts), culture and values as stated, recent news (last 6 months), leadership names relevant to the role.
2. **Role intelligence:** why the role is open if stated, team and likely manager if public, what success looks like in the first 90 days (inferred from the posting, marked as inference), how this role differs from similar titles elsewhere.
3. **Hiring process:** stages and typical format if sources describe them; otherwise say it is not known. Do not present a guess as fact.
4. **Pay and terms intelligence:** sourced market range for the role, level and location type, with sources and dates. See `pay-check.md`.
5. **Reputation and risk:** employee and customer feedback from reputable public sources, labelled as opinion and as possibly unrepresentative. Note legitimacy findings.
6. **Likely questions with answers:** about fifteen questions with STAR answers built only from the user's real evidence (`interview-pack.md`), plus the hardest three and how to handle the user's gaps honestly.
7. **Become the best candidate:** the top gaps between the posting and the user, and a 48-hour and a one-week plan to close the cheapest ones (a small project, a certificate, a rewrite of a portfolio item). The user's evidence must stay true: plans are for real work they will actually do.
8. **Sharp questions to ask them** (three), drawn from the research.
9. **Two-minute pitch** and a one-line positioning statement, truthful.

## Honesty
This is research, not inside information. Review sites and paywalled sources may not be readable; say so. Never invent an interviewer, a process or a quote. Cite each source as a link.
